Third party software in these builds
====================================

The release packages contain no files of the original game. Copy the
executable and the files next to it into your own installation of the game.

The following libraries are part of the packages. Their license texts are in
this folder.

librw (MIT license, licenses/librw.txt)
    Linked into the executable on every platform.
    Source: https://github.com/aap/librw

OpenAL Soft (GNU LGPL, licenses/openal-soft.txt)
    Shipped as a separate library: OpenAL32.dll on Windows, libopenal.*.dylib
    on macOS. You may replace it with your own build of OpenAL Soft.
    Source: https://github.com/kcat/openal-soft

mpg123 (GNU LGPL 2.1, licenses/mpg123.txt)
    Shipped as a separate library: libmpg123-0.dll on Windows,
    libmpg123.*.dylib on macOS. You may replace it with your own build.
    Source: https://www.mpg123.de/

GLFW (zlib license, licenses/glfw.txt)
    Used by the OpenGL builds: linked into the Windows executable, shipped as
    libglfw.*.dylib on macOS.
    Source: https://www.glfw.org/

The Linux builds use the OpenAL Soft, mpg123 and GLFW libraries installed on
your system.
